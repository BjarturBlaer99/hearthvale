# Eight days played: the quarry beside the plot

The report of phase 3b of mining and crafting (`docs/progress/mining-crafting-plan.md`). **No price and no
count has been changed.** The last section says which numbers I would change; the tuning waits for the
user's word.

## How it was played

- **A script played eight days of a new game in a real window, at life's own speed,** on 2026-11-17:
  `tools/play_days.gd`. It pressed keys and mouse buttons while the game ran as it does for a player:
  1813 seconds of play, thirty minutes.
- **It started at the title screen** and took "New game". Its save was slot 956, emptied before and after.
  The player's own save has the same checksum as before.
- **It looks at the game only to decide what to press:** which cells can be walked, where rocks stand and
  what shows in them, where monsters are, what a list offers. It never reads where the ladder is hidden.
- **It changed the game's state once:** the Lantern was put in its pack on the morning of day 7.
- **Three earlier runs at four times speed were trials of the script.** Their numbers are used below only
  where it says so, to show how much a day can differ.

## The short of it

- **The plan's first guesses were about right for a new miner, and far off for a seasoned one. He is
  seasoned by day 4.**
  - Day 2, the plain Pickaxe: floors 1 to 3 gave 16 copper ore for 94 energy. The plan said 15 for about
    130.
  - Day 7, the Iron Pickaxe, Mining at its top and the Lantern: all nine floors gave 261 coins of ore for
    115 energy. The plan said 195 coins for about 405 energy at the start.
- **Mining reached its top level on the second morning** (day 2, 11:20), and **the Iron Pickaxe was made
  on the fourth.**
- **From then, floors 1 to 6 cost less than one bar of energy and pay about 110 coins a day.** Day 5: 18
  copper ore and 11 iron ore, 109 coins, for 85 energy and one loaf.
- **The plot paid 204 coins every three days** (17 turnips), 40 a day once the seed is paid for, for about
  10 energy a day.
- **The two do not compete for the day.** The plot takes a few minutes and a tenth of the bar. The longest
  day, all nine floors, was over at 15:40.
- **Early, the quarry pays in tools, not in coins.** He went to bed with fewer than ten coins on every one
  of the first seven days. On day 8 he sold everything he had for 571.
- **The dark floors are worth nothing without the Lantern and the most with it.**
  - Day 3, no light: a Bogwisp touched him at the foot of the ladder and threw him back up it. He went
    down again, was touched once more, and left with 3 gold ore.
  - Day 7, with the Lantern: 9 gold ore and 4 crystals, 150 coins, for 36 energy and not one touch.
- **Coal and stone pile up.** He ended with 25 coal and 50 stone (a full stack) after the furnace had
  burned on five nights.
- **The furnace is the narrow place:** five bars a night. Day 7's ore was twelve bars' worth, so most ore
  is sold as it is.

## What the script set up by hand, and what it left out

**Set up by hand**
- **The Lantern,** put in his pack on the morning of day 7. The story gives it in the crypt, the last
  dungeon, which eight days of plot and quarry don't reach.
- **Nothing else.** Every coin, tool, bar and skill point was come by in play.

**Left out**
- **The story:** Elder Bram, the keep, the crypt. So no bow, no heart containers, three hearts throughout.
- **The two new favours, and every other favour.** Three marks take about a week of gifts, and he gave
  none. He talked to Tilda every day, and she taught her recipe on day 8. The favours are covered by
  tests and shown in two pictures.
- **Wood, the chest, the lamp and the rug:** he cut no branch.
- **The iron hoe and can, and the Miner's Lantern.**
- **Cooking and gathering:** all he ate was Tilda's bread.
- **Every crop but turnips.**
- **Sprinting:** he walks everywhere, so no energy went on it.
- **The heart piece on floor 9.**

**Where the script is not a player**
- **It sees rocks in the dark.** A player without a light sees a cell or so round him.
- **It knows what a monster is about to do** the moment it begins. A player sees the same tell a little
  later.
- **It does not dodge arrows,** and fights only what comes into reach.
- **It never hesitates, and never explores.**

**Early (days 1 to 3)**
- **A day in the quarry is hungry and pays little.** Day 2 took 182 energy, three loaves with it, for 83
  coins of ore. That is 2 energy a coin, where the plan guessed 3.
- **None of that ore became coins.** It became the furnace (day 2), the Copper Pickaxe (day 3) and the
  Iron Pickaxe (day 4). He sold only what bread cost.
- **The plot paid nothing yet either:** five turnips, 60 coins, on day 4.
- **So early there is no contest:** both are slow, and the quarry is where the tools come from.

**Late (from day 4 or 5)**
- **Floors 1 to 6 are one bar of energy and about 110 coins.** Day 5: 85 energy, 109 coins. Day 7: 79
  energy, 111 coins.
- **With the Lantern, all nine are 261 coins for 115 energy** and three loaves (18 coins).
- **The plot is 40 coins a day for about 10 energy** with turnips. The plan's sum for pumpkins is 90.
- **A coin costs about the same energy from either:** 0.3 to 0.5 from the plot, 0.4 from the whole mine,
  0.7 to 0.8 from floors 1 to 6. The plan expected the mine to cost three to nine times as much.
- **There are far more coins in the mine.** 261 against 40: six plots of turnips, or three of pumpkins,
  and he can tend the plot the same morning.
- **A loaf is what turns into ore.** Late, 6 coins of bread are 30 energy, and 30 energy are about 40
  coins of ore on floors 1 to 6.

**So: is the mine clearly ahead?** Yes, from the day the Iron Pickaxe is made, and by a long way once he
has the Lantern. The plan put it at "200 coins or more, twice a plot of pumpkins". It measured 261, nearly
three times, for a bar and a bit of energy.

**How sure is this?** The safe floors are steady: in the three trials floors 1 to 6 with the Iron Pickaxe
gave between 96 and 129 coins. The dark floors are not. In the last trial, with the Lantern, he fell once on floor 8 and came home with 4 gold ore and 1 crystal: 55 coins, not 150.

## What I would change, and why

Nothing below has been changed. Each is a number in data.

**1. What Tilda pays: a third off, as the plan foresaw.**

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Copper ore | 3 | 2 |
| Iron ore | 5 | 3 |
| Gold ore | 10 | 7 |
| Crystal | 15 | 10 |
| Copper bar | 12 | 8 |
| Iron bar | 20 | 12 |
| Gold bar | 36 | 25 |

- **Why:** it puts a day on floors 1 to 6 between a plot of turnips and a plot of pumpkins, where the plan
  meant one band to sit. A bar is still worth more than its three ore.
- **What the run's own ore would have paid:**

| | Now | Proposed |
|---|---|---|
| Day 2 (plain Pickaxe, floors 1 to 5) | 83 | 53 |
| Day 5 (Iron Pickaxe, floors 1 to 6) | 109 | 69 |
| Day 7 (the Lantern, all nine) | 261 | 173 |
| A plot of turnips, a day | 40 | 40 |
| A plot of pumpkins, a day (the plan's sum) | 90 | 90 |

- **What it leaves:** with the Lantern the whole mine still pays about two plots of pumpkins. The Lantern
  is the last dungeon's prize, so that may be as it should. If not, gold ore and crystals are the lever:
  the dark floors are 150 of day 7's 261.

**2. Mining rises too fast: a rock of ore 2 points to 1, a plain rock 1 point to none.**
- **Why:** the top level came on the second morning. Farming was at 4 after eight days.
- **What it does:** the top level takes 90 rocks of ore or coal. He would have reached level 3 on day 2,
  level 4 on day 3 and the top on day 5. It can't be made slower than that in data: a deed gives a whole
  point or none.

**3. Coal on floors 4, 5 and 6: 4 rocks a floor to 2.**
- **Why:** those floors gave 13 coal a day. The furnace burns five a night at the most, Tilda doesn't buy
  it, and he ended with 25. With 2 a floor a day gives 6 or 7, and floors 7 and 8 have 4 more.

**Worth a thought, and yours to call**
- **The Iron Pickaxe waits on Mining 3, which he had on the first day.** It came on day 4, and it is what
  makes floors 1 to 6 a one-bar job. With change 2, asking Mining 5 for it would hold it back to about
  day 6.
- **The furnace's five bars a night.** Day 7's ore was twelve bars' worth. If bars are meant to be how ore
  is sold, eight a night would let them be.

**What I would leave alone**
- **Bread at 6 coins, the Pickaxe at 25, and 2 energy a blow.** The first three days felt right in the
  numbers: poor, hungry, and every day a new tool.
- **The favours' rewards.** At the proposed prices Edda's two iron bars are 24 coins of iron for 50 coins
  and a pie, which is generous as a favour should be.

## Seen on the way, and not numbers

- **A Bogwisp's touch at the foot of the ladder throws him back up it.** He arrives one cell below the
  way up, and a knock northward walks him onto it. It happened on day 3, and in three of the trial runs.
- **With the Lantern, a Bogwisp that flares can be struck before it darts.** It stands still for half a
  second, inside the Lantern's light. Running at it and striking twice beats it with no dart at all. That
  is how day 7 took no touch in the dark. Before the script did this, it took four or five touches a floor.
- **A fall in the quarry costs nothing but the walk,** and with the lift not even that. It also heals him.
- **Combat rose to its top level in seven days** without his seeking a fight: 96 monsters, most of them
  the North Fields' slimes, which are back on every visit, and he passes them on every walk to the quarry
  and back.
- **Stone has one use after the furnace:** bombs.
- **The line "Carrying:" in the corner was cut off all week.** With ore, bars and tools in the pack it
  shows the first three things.
- **Tilda's door is shut until 08:00,** and every mining day began with a wait of an hour and a half
  outside it for bread.

## What this run can't tell

- **How any of it feels.** The script shows what the numbers do, not whether a floor is a pleasure.
- **How a person fares in the dark.** The script is quicker than a hand at a Bogwisp's flare.
- **A second week.** Pumpkins, the chest, the iron hoe and can, and the Miner's Lantern were not reached.
- **One run is one run.** The trials show the safe floors are steady and the dark floors are not.
